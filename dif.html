CONFIRM

Before start: records on time.
After start: begins now.

PAUSE

Finalizes the recording. It cannot be resumed.

WHAT GETS RECORDED
The content sent to the projector, plus the selected camera view. Or the content sent to the LEFT projector, if the room has multiple.
CAMERA VIEW
Select instructor, students, or both. Both is one combined side-by-side view, not separate feeds.
WHERE RECORDINGS GO
Recordings upload to My Media in Canvas. Scheduled ones also show up as pending in Course Media for you to review and publish.
